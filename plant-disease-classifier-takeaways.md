## Misc learnings
a) Pytorch expects the inputs in floats as part of training
b) Converting only the batch to float during learning leads to an about 4x smaller memory footprint.


## Theoritical learnings
a) CNN's not only train faster, get superior performance for image classification tasks. For example: A CNN had a validation set accuracy of 87% compared to a 68% accurate fully connected neural net, even though the fully connected neural net had 393,248,015 parameters compared to CNN just having 937,299 parameters.
